
# 🤝 AI VISION HUB — CODE OF CONDUCT

## Our Pledge

We are committed to making participation in AI Vision Hub a welcoming, respectful, and harassment-free experience for everyone.

We welcome contributions from people of all backgrounds, experience levels, and identities.

## Our Standards

Examples of behavior that contribute to a positive environment include:

- Being respectful and considerate.
- Welcoming different perspectives and experiences.
- Giving and accepting constructive feedback.
- Taking responsibility for mistakes.
- Focusing on what is best for the project and community.
- Showing empathy toward other contributors.

Examples of unacceptable behavior include:

- Harassment, discrimination, or personal attacks.
- Trolling, insults, or derogatory comments.
- Publishing another person's private information without permission.
- Deliberately disrupting discussions or contributions.
- Unwelcome sexual attention or advances.
- Any conduct that would be inappropriate in a professional community.

## Our Responsibilities

Project maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing these standards.

They may remove, edit, or reject contributions, comments, commits, and other project activity that violates this Code of Conduct.

Maintainers may take appropriate action in response to unacceptable behavior.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies within project spaces, including:

- GitHub issues
- Pull requests
- Discussions
- Project communication channels
- Other spaces where someone represents AI Vision Hub

## Reporting

If you experience or witness unacceptable behavior, contact the project maintainer privately through an appropriate communication channel.

Please provide:

1. A description of the incident.
2. Relevant dates and context.
3. Links or supporting evidence, if available.

Do not publish private information or sensitive evidence in public issues.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers will review reports and determine appropriate action based on the circumstances.

Possible actions include:

- A warning.
- Removal of inappropriate content.
- Temporary restrictions on participation.
- Permanent removal from project spaces.
